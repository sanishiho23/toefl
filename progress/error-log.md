# Error Log

**The most valuable file in this repository.** Every wrong answer goes here, with a reason.
A mistake you can't name is a mistake you'll repeat.

---

## How to write a row

| Column | What goes in it | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Date | yyyy-mm-dd | 2026-10-06 |
| Src | Where the question came from | `S01 ex #4` · `Struct Test 01 #22` · `Mock #1 RC #31` |
| Category | The rule or type | `S01 decoy 1 — prep phrase` |
| Why | **The actual reason.** Not "careless." | `grabbed "study" instead of "results"` |
| Fix | One concrete action | `underline the headword before choosing` |
| ✓ | Retested correctly? | `✔` |

> ⚠️ **"Careless" is not a reason.** If you wrote it, dig one level deeper: was it speed?
> A rule you half-know? Misreading the question stem? Name it or it comes back.

## Category codes — use these, they make patterns visible

**Structure (S01–S08)**
- `S01` agreement · decoys: 1 prep phrase · 2 inversion · 3 one-of · 4 gerund subj · 5 quantity · 6 collective · 7 -ics/plural · 8 correlative
- `S02` tense · `S02-voice` passive · `S02-modal` modal passive
- `S03` participle-ed/-ing · `S03-ger` gerund vs infinitive · `S03-caus` causative · `S03-dang` dangling modifier
- `S04` noun clause order · `S04-subj` subjunctive · `S04-report` reported speech
- `S05` relative pronoun · `S05-comma` restrictive/non-restrictive · `S05-reduce` reduction
- `S06` connector · `S06-prep` conjunction vs preposition · `S06-cond` conditional
- `S07` inversion · `S07-comp` comparative · `S07-par` parallelism
- `S08` word form · `S08-art` article · `S08-prep` preposition · `S08-pro` pronoun · `S08-red` redundancy

**Listening (L01–L04)**
- `L-A-func` function/idiom · `L-A-neg` hidden negative · `L-A-infer` inference
- `L-B-num` number missed · `L-B-sugg` suggestion
- `L-C-main` main topic · `L-C-att` attitude · `L-C-struct` organisation
- `L-lost` lost focus / didn't let go

**Reading (R01–R03)**
- `R-main` · `R-detail` · `R-infer` · `R-except` · `R-vocab` · `R-ref` · `R-tone` · `R-insert`
- `R-time` ran out of time · `R-misread` misread the stem

**Cross-cutting**
- `T-time` timing · `T-guess` left blank / bad guess · `T-focus` attention drift
