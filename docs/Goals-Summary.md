## Overall Goal (6 weeks)

Score **550** on TOEFL ITP Level 1. Because the total is
`round((Listening + Structure + Reading) × 10 / 3)`, that means the three section scores must
**add to 165**. The practical reading of that is roughly **105 correct answers out of 140**.

The plan is built on three claims:

1. **Structure is the fastest place to buy points.** It is ~15 recurring rules, it is the
   shortest section, and it is the reported weakest area. It gets the first two weeks.
2. **Reading and Listening each need a dedicated week** because they are skills, not rule sets.
3. **The last two weeks are for integration and sleep**, not for new content.
