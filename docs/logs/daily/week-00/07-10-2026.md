### Reflection

1. **What went well:** Clean on markers that have a single, unambiguous signal: *in 1999* → simple past (#4), *yet* → present perfect (#10), interrupted action → past progressive (#11), *since* + point in time (#12). The rules are in my head; the problem is applying them under time pressure.
2. **What didn't:** 58% is below target. The misses cluster around two failure modes: (a) seeing a past-tense marker and defaulting to simple past instead of checking if there's an earlier past event, and (b) missing passive voice when the subject receives the action. Also: *by* + future time + duration is not yet automatic.
3. **Tomorrow's first move:** Before the Day 8 mixed drill, write a two-step card: (1) "Find the time marker first" and (2) "Does the subject do the action, or receive it?" Read it aloud, then start the timer.
