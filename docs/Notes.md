## Topic 1 — the score arithmetic

`Total = round((Listening + Structure + Reading) × 10 / 3)`.

Check: minimum 31+31+31 = 93 → 310. Maximum 68+68+67 = 203 → 677. Both match ETS.

**550 requires the three section scores to sum to 165.**
Everything in this repository is aimed at that one number.

## Topic 2 — the slot test (Section 2)

Before reading the options, name what the blank needs:

| Slot | Needs |
|---|---|
| after `a / an / the / my / this` | noun |
| after `very / more / most / extremely` | adjective |
| modifying a verb, adjective or sentence | adverb |
| the sentence has no action | verb (+ tense) |

Then match. This removes most of the guessing from word-form questions.

## Topic 3 — the five decoys (subject-verb agreement)

1. Prepositional phrase between subject and verb
2. Inverted sentence (`there ___`, `on the wall ___`)
3. `one of the` vs `the only one of the`
4. Gerund or infinitive as subject
5. Quantity expressions (`the number of` vs `a number of`)

Plus: collective nouns singular, `-ics` nouns singular, `either…or` agrees with the nearest.

## Topic 4 — the Part A rule

If the second speaker's reply does not literally answer the question, stop asking what it
*means* and ask what the speaker is *doing*: agreeing, refusing, suggesting, surprised,
hedging, correcting, dismissing.

---

## Topic 5 — the reading attack order

vocabulary → detail → NOT/EXCEPT → reference → inference → main idea → tone → insert.

Cheap points first. Write "NOT" in large letters the moment you see EXCEPT.

---

## Topic 6 — the 40-second rule

If no answer has been chosen in 40 seconds, you do not know it. Eliminate one option, guess,
mark, move on. An unanswered question is 0; a guess is 25%. There is no guessing penalty.

---

## Topic 7 — timing checkpoints

Structure: 20 questions by 12:30 remaining, 30 by 6:00 remaining.
Reading: past question 25 at 30:00 remaining, past 38 at 15:00 remaining.

## Topic 8 — what to write in the error log

`source → category → why → fix`. **"Careless" is not a reason.** Dig one level deeper:
speed, a half-known rule, or a misread stem. Name it or it comes back.
