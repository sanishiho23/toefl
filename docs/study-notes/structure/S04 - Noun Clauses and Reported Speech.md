# S04 — Noun Clauses & Reported Speech

**Section:** Structure & Written Expression · **Est. yield:** 3–5 questions
**Time to learn:** 25 min

---

## The one rule

> A noun clause is **`that / what / whether / if / wh-word` + SUBJECT + VERB**.
> Never question word order. Never.

```
❌ I don't know where is the library.
✔  I don't know where the library is.

❌ She asked me when would I arrive.
✔  She asked me when I would arrive.
```
This single rule accounts for about a third of all noun-clause questions on the test.

---

## 1. The connectors

| Connector | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `that` | statement | I believe **that** the data is reliable. |
| `what` | the thing which (acts as a noun *inside* the clause) | **What** he said surprised us. |
| `whether / if` | yes-no question | I wonder **whether** she will come. |
| `who / whom / which / whose` | who or what | Tell me **who** called. |
| `when / where / why / how` | time, place, reason, manner | Explain **how** it works. |

⚠️ **`that` vs `what`:** use `what` when the clause needs its own subject or object.
- ✔ *I know that he left.* (clause already complete)
- ✔ *I know what he left.* (what = the thing that he left)

⚠️ **`whether` vs `if`:** `if` cannot follow a preposition.
- ❌ *It depends on if it rains.* ✔ *It depends on whether it rains.*

---

## 2. `that`-clause as subject or object
```
___ the experiment failed was unexpected.      → That    (never "What" here — clause is complete)
The results suggest ___ the hypothesis is wrong. → that
```
`that` may be **omitted** after *say, think, believe, know, suggest, show, indicate* —
but **never** when the clause is the subject, and never after a noun.

---

## 3. The subjunctive — a guaranteed question

After verbs of **demand / suggestion / necessity**:
`suggest, recommend, demand, require, request, insist, propose, advise, urge, ask, order`

> **verb + that + subject + BASE VERB** (no -s, no *to*, no tense)

```
The committee recommended that she ___ the proposal.
(A) revises  (B) revised  (C) revise  (D) to revise     → C
```

Same pattern after impersonal adjectives:
`It is essential / important / necessary / vital / imperative / advisable / crucial + that + base verb`
```
It is essential that every applicant ___ on time.       → be
```

---

## 4. Reported speech — tense shifts

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| present simple | past simple |
| present progressive | past progressive |
| present perfect | past perfect |
| past simple | past perfect (or unchanged) |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| may | might |
| must | had to |

**Time/place words shift too:** today → that day · tomorrow → the next day ·
yesterday → the day before · here → there · this → that · ago → before.

---

## 5. Verb + object + infinitive
`want, ask, tell, expect, encourage, force, invite, remind, warn, persuade, allow, advise, permit`
```
She asked me ___ the report by Friday.                  → to finish
```
⚠️ **Exceptions (no *to*):** `make, let, have` + object + base verb.

---

## 6. `whether or not` & `no matter`
```
___ he agrees or not, the project will continue.        → Whether
___ how difficult it is, she never gives up.            → No matter
```

## Exercise — Retake (12 items, 7 minutes)

1. The doctor advised the patient ___ more water daily.
   (A) drink  (B) drinking  (C) to drink  (D) drank
2. It is important that the report ___ submitted on time.
   (A) is  (B) was  (C) be  (D) will be
3. She said that she ___ the assignment the previous night.
   (A) finishes  (B) finished  (C) had finished  (D) has finished
4. Do you know when ___ ?
   (A) does the library close  (B) the library closes
   (C) closes the library  (D) the library does close
5. The director demanded that every employee ___ the new policy.
   (A) reads  (B) read  (C) will read  (D) is reading
6. He mentioned that he ___ to London the following month.
   (A) will travel  (B) travels  (C) would travel  (D) is traveling
7. ___ surprised everyone was that the team finished ahead of schedule.
   (A) That  (B) What  (C) Whether  (D) It
8. The success of the project depends on ___ the budget is approved.
   (A) if  (B) that  (C) whether  (D) what
9. The teacher told the class ___ their textbooks to page 50.
   (A) open  (B) opening  (C) to open  (D) opened
10. ___ caused the power outage is still under investigation.
    (A) That  (B) Whether  (C) What  (D) If
11. The witness claimed that he ___ nothing unusual that evening.
    (A) sees  (B) has seen  (C) had seen  (D) will see
12. Nobody knows ___ the conference will be held next year.
    (A) that  (B) what  (C) whether  (D) if
