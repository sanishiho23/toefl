# S07 — Inversion, Comparatives & Parallelism

**Section:** Structure & Written Expression · **Est. yield:** 3–4 questions
**Time to learn:** 25 min

---

## 1. Inversion — when the verb jumps in front of the subject

**Trigger:** a negative or limiting word at the **front** of the clause.

`never · rarely · seldom · hardly · scarcely · barely · not only · no sooner ·
little · nowhere · only after · only then · only when · only if · not until ·
under no circumstances · in no way · at no time`

**Pattern:** `trigger + auxiliary + subject + main verb`

```
Never ___ such a remarkable result.
(A) I have seen  (B) have I seen  (C) I saw  (D) did I saw        → B

Not only ___ the deadline, but she also exceeded the target.
(A) she met  (B) did she meet  (C) she did meet  (D) met she      → B
```
⚠️ If the trigger is **not** at the front of the clause, there is **no inversion**:
*She never has seen such a result.* → no inversion needed after the subject.

**Other inversions you'll meet:**
- `so + adjective` at the front → *So difficult was the exam that half the class failed.*
- `so / neither / nor` for agreement → *She passed, and so did he.* / *He didn't, and neither did I.*
- place adverb at the front → *On the wall hangs a portrait.*
- conditional inversion → *Had I known… / Were she here… / Should you need…* (see S06)

## 2. Comparatives

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| short adjective + `-er … than` | cheaper than |
| more + long adjective + than | more expensive than |
| as + adjective + as | as reliable as |
| not so/as … as | not as fast as |
| the + comparative, the + comparative | The sooner, the better. |
| comparative + and + comparative | It is getting colder and colder. |
| double the / twice as … as | twice as large as |
| `than` + pronoun in formal writing | taller than I (am) |

**Irregulars:** good/better/best · bad/worse/worst · far/farther|further/farthest|furthest ·
little/less/least · many|much/more/most

⚠️ **Never double-mark:** ❌ *more better* ❌ *more cheaper* ❌ *the most fastest*

**Superlative:** `the + -est / the most …` + `of` or `in`
```
the tallest building in the city · the most reliable of the three methods
```
⚠️ ❌ *most unique* — *unique, perfect, absolute, essential* are already absolute.

## 3. Parallelism — everything joined must match

> Anything connected by **and / or / but / both…and / not only…but also / either…or /
> neither…nor / rather than** must have the **same grammatical shape**.

```
❌ She enjoys reading, hiking, and to swim.
✔  She enjoys reading, hiking, and swimming.

❌ The report was thorough but it lacked clarity.
✔  The report was thorough but unclear.
```

**Correlatives must bracket equal elements:**
```
❌ She is both intelligent and has great discipline.
✔  She is both intelligent and disciplined.
```

**Comparisons must compare like with like:**
```
❌ The salary of a professor is higher than a teacher.
✔  The salary of a professor is higher than that of a teacher.
❌ Her research is more detailed than him.
✔  Her research is more detailed than his.
```

---

## 4. `like` vs `as`
- **like** = similar to (preposition, + noun phrase)
- **as** = in the same way / in the role of (conjunction, + clause)

```
She works ___ a researcher.        → as     (role)
She sings ___ a professional.      → like   (similarity)
```

## Exercise — 12 items, 7 minutes

1. Rarely ___ such a well-designed experiment.
   (A) we have seen  (B) have we seen  (C) we saw  (D) did we saw
2. Not only ___ on time, but the quality was excellent.
   (A) the report was  (B) was the report  (C) the report  (D) it was
3. The results were ___ than anyone had predicted.
   (A) more better  (B) more good  (C) better  (D) gooder
4. The harder you practise, ___ you become.
   (A) the more confident  (B) more confident  (C) the most confident  (D) confident
5. The professor asked the students to read carefully, take notes, and ___ .
   (A) to summarize the text  (B) summarizing the text  (C) summarize the text  (D) summarized
6. No sooner ___ the room than the lights went out.
   (A) she entered  (B) had she entered  (C) she had entered  (D) did she entered
7. The population of the city is larger than ___ of the surrounding towns.
   (A) those  (B) that  (C) these  (D) it
8. She is ___ intelligent and hard-working.
   (A) both  (B) either  (C) neither  (D) not only
9. Only after the data ___ did they publish their findings.
   (A) was verified  (B) were they verified  (C) verified  (D) they verified
10. The new method is ___ as the old one.
    (A) twice so efficient  (B) twice as efficient  (C) as twice efficient  (D) two times so efficient
11. He works ___ a research assistant in the biology department.
    (A) like  (B) as  (C) so  (D) similar
12. The committee valued honesty ___ than diplomacy.
    (A) more highly  (B) more high  (C) most highly  (D) higher more
