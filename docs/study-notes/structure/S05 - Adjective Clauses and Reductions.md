# S05 — Adjective Clauses & Reductions

**Section:** Structure & Written Expression · **Est. yield:** 3–5 questions
**Time to learn:** 25 min

---

## 1. Choosing the relative pronoun

| Pronoun | Refers to | Function in the clause |
|---|---|---|
| **who** | people | subject |
| **whom** | people | object (after a preposition or verb) |
| **which** | things, animals | subject or object |
| **that** | people **or** things | subject or object (restrictive only) |
| **whose** | possession (people & things) | determiner |
| **where / when / why** | place / time / reason | adverbial |

```
The researcher ___ discovered the effect retired last year.        → who
The lab ___ she works in was renovated.                            → where (or which)
The scientist ___ work we cited will be visiting.                  → whose
```

---

## 2. Restrictive vs non-restrictive — the comma rule

- **Restrictive** (needed to identify): **no commas**, `that` or `which` OK.
- **Non-restrictive** (extra info): **commas**, `which` only — **never `that`**.

```
The book that I borrowed is overdue.        (no commas — which book?)
My thesis, which took three years, is done. (commas — extra information)
```
⚠️ `that` after a comma is one of the most-tested Written Expression errors.

---

## 3. Reduction — turning a clause into a phrase

| Clause type | Reduced form |
|---|---|
| active, ongoing or habitual | `-ing` phrase |
| passive | past participle |
| verb `be` + adjective/noun/prepositional phrase | drop `subject + be` |

```
The students who are sitting in the front row … → The students sitting in the front row …
The data which was collected last year …        → The data collected last year …
The man who is responsible for the project …    → The man responsible for the project …
```

⚠️ **Cannot reduce** when the clause contains a modal (*can, will, must, should*) or a perfect verb
(*has done, had been*). Those items must keep the full clause.

⚠️ **Dangling reduction:** the reduced phrase must modify the noun right next to it.
```
❌ Published in 1998, many scientists cited the paper.   (scientists weren't published)
✔  Published in 1998, the paper was widely cited.
```

---

## 4. Quantity + relative pronoun
```
The committee has twelve members, three of ___ are from abroad.  → whom
The report contains six chapters, the last of ___ is a summary.  → which
```
Pattern: `number / some / many / most / none / all / half + of + whom | which`

---

## 5. Friendly traps

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| the reason why … is because | the reason … is **that** | *because* is redundant after *reason* |
| the person which | the person **who/that** | people take *who* |
| in where | **where** (or *in which*) | *where* already contains the preposition |
| that's the man who I saw him | …who I saw *(no him)* | the pronoun repeats the subject |
| I have a friend lives in Osaka | …who lives / …living | a clause needs a connector |

## Exercise — Retake (12 items, 7 minutes)

1. The scientist ___ research was published in Nature received an award.
   (A) who  (B) whom  (C) whose  (D) which
2. The data, ___ were collected over five years, support the hypothesis.
   (A) that  (B) which  (C) what  (D) they
3. The applicants, most of ___ had graduate degrees, were highly qualified.
   (A) who  (B) which  (C) whom  (D) them
4. The books ___ from the library must be returned by Friday.
   (A) borrowing  (B) borrowed  (C) were borrowed  (D) which borrowing
5. The park ___ we used to play has been turned into a parking lot.
   (A) which  (B) where  (C) that  (D) whose
6. The files ___ on the desk belong to the director.
   (A) laying  (B) lain  (C) lying  (D) laid
7. That is the hospital at ___ my mother works.
   (A) where  (B) that  (C) which  (D) who
8. The volunteers, ___ helped during the flood, were honored by the mayor.
   (A) that  (B) which  (C) who  (D) whom
9. The reason he was late is ___ his car broke down.
   (A) because  (B) why  (C) that  (D) due to
10. ___ written in pencil will not be accepted.
    (A) Answers that  (B) Answers which  (C) Answers  (D) Answers are
11. The town ___ in the 18th century is now a tourist attraction.
    (A) founding  (B) founded  (C) was founded  (D) having founded
12. The artist, none of ___ paintings had sold, decided to change styles.
    (A) who  (B) whom  (C) whose  (D) which
