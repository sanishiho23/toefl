# R02 — Vocabulary in Context & Word Attack

**Section:** Reading + Structure (S08) · **Est. yield:** 10–15 questions across the test

> Vocabulary is not a separate skill you study on Sunday. It is the substrate of both the
> Reading section and Written Expression. **20 words a day for 40 days = 800 words.**
> That is the difference between 480 and 560.

---

## Part 1 — The substitution test (for vocabulary-in-context questions)

1. **Cover the four options.**
2. Read the sentence containing the word.
3. Supply your own English word for the blank.
4. Uncover the options and find the closest match.

Why: the options are usually all *valid dictionary meanings* of the word. Only one matches
*this* sentence. If you read the options first, four reasonable-looking words hijack you.

```
The senator's argument was tenuous at best.
(A) lengthy  (B) weak  (C) detailed  (D) controversial
```
Own word: *weak / thin*. → **B**. (All four could describe an argument.)

## Part 2 — Context clues

| Clue type | Signal | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Definition** | *which is, that is, namely, defined as, refers to* | *Photosynthesis, **that is**, the conversion of light into chemical energy…* |
| **Restatement** | dashes, parentheses, commas, *or* | *The artifact — a ceremonial blade — was intact.* |
| **Contrast** | *but, however, although, whereas, rather than, unlike, on the other hand* | *Unlike his **garrulous** brother, he was silent.* → talkative |
| **Cause–effect** | *because, so, therefore, as a result, thus* | *The soil was **barren**, so nothing grew.* → infertile |
| **Example** | *for example, such as, including, like* | *Many **ruminants**, such as cattle and sheep…* |
| **Synonym/parallel** | *and, or, similarly, likewise* | *confused and **perplexed*** |
| **Word structure** | prefix + root + suffix | *un-believ-able* |

## Part 6 — The 20-minute daily routine

| Min | Task |
|---:|---|
| 5 | 20 new words from [Academic Word List](<../vocabulary/[20-Oct] Academic Word List.md>). Word + definition + your own sentence. |
| 5 | Review yesterday's 20. |
| 5 | Review the 20 from two days ago (spaced repetition day 2). |
| 5 | Review the 20 from seven days ago (spaced repetition day 7). |

**Spaced-repetition schedule:** every word is seen on day 0, 1, 2, 7, 21. That's it. Five
exposures is enough for recognition-level knowledge, which is all the test requires.

## Part 7 — Words that signal the author's position

Learn these; they decide tone questions and inference questions.

| Favourable | Unfavourable | Hedging | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| significant, valuable, remarkable, elegant, robust, promising | questionable, problematic, flawed, dubious, inadequate, superficial | apparently, presumably, seemingly, to some extent, arguably | crucially, notably, in fact, above all, fundamentally |
