# Everyday & Practical English (E-series)

Supplementary modules covering **everyday English** that supports the test (spoken numbers,
prepositions of place, weather, business vocabulary) plus one off-test enrichment page.

Use these as **warm-up material** (the 5-min warm-up block in the
[session anatomy](<../../curriculum/README.md#session-anatomy--every-day-follows-this-shape>)),
not as a replacement for the S/L/R core modules.

| # | Module | On the ITP? | Pays off in |
|---|---|---|---|
| E01 | [Numbers, Decimals & Quantities](<E01 - Numbers, Decimals and Quantities.md>) ⭐ | Yes | Listening (prices, dates, stats) |
| E02 | [Directions & Places](<E02 - Directions and Places.md>) ⭐ | Yes | Listening Part A + Structure S08 |
| E03 | [Weather & Seasons](<E03 - Weather and Seasons.md>) | Partly | Listening small talk, Reading |
| E04 | [Business & Workplace English](<E04 - Business and Workplace English.md>) | Partly | Reading, vocabulary |
| E05 | [Gen Z Slang](<E05 - Gen Z Slang - Off-Test.md>) 🚫 | **No** | Real-life casual only — not the test |

**Priority:** E01 and E02 first (they directly convert to points). E03/E04 are breadth.
E05 is off-test — read it on a rest day only.
