# Everyday & Practical English (E-series)

Supplementary modules covering **everyday English** that supports the test (spoken numbers,
prepositions of place, weather, business vocabulary) plus one off-test enrichment page.

Use these as **warm-up material** (the 5-min warm-up block in the
[session anatomy](<../../curriculum/README.md#session-anatomy--every-day-follows-this-shape>)),
not as a replacement for the S/L/R core modules.

| # | Module | On the ITP? | Pays off in |
|---|---|---|---|
| E01 | [Numbers, Decimals & Quantities](<E01 - Numbers, Decimals and Quantities.md>) ⭐ | Yes | Listening (prices, dates, stats) |
| E02 | [Directions & Places](<E02 - Directions and Places.md>) ⭐ | Yes | Listening Part A + Structure S08 |
| E03 | [Weather & Seasons](<E03 - Weather and Seasons.md>) | Partly | Listening small talk, Reading |
| E04 | [Business & Workplace English](<E04 - Business and Workplace English.md>) | Partly | Reading, vocabulary |
| E05 | [Gen Z Slang](<E05 - Gen Z Slang - Off-Test.md>) 🚫 | **No** | Real-life casual only — not the test |
| E06 | [Travel & Hospitality](<E06 - Travel and Hospitality.md>) | Yes | Listening Part A (service conversations) |
| E07 | [Phone & Everyday Communication](<E07 - Phone and Everyday Communication.md>) | Yes | Listening Part A, workplace |
| E08 | [Everyday Collocations: do/make/have/take](<E08 - Everyday Collocations - do, make, have, take.md>) ⭐ | Yes | Listening, Structure, Reading |

**Priority:** E01, E02 and E08 first (they directly convert to points). E06/E07 cover the
service-conversation settings common in Listening Part A. E03/E04 are breadth.
E05 is off-test — read it on a rest day only.

## Further practice (external, optional)

Free or reference resources for extra everyday-English practice. **None is required** — the
modules above are self-contained. Use these only if you want more drilling.

| Resource | What it is | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| [Oxford *English File* phrasebank](https://elt.oup.com/student/englishfile/intplus3/phrasebank/) | Interactive "practical English" video scenarios (check-in, ordering, directions) | Free |
| *Practical English Usage* (Michael Swan, OUP) | The standard grammar/usage reference guide — look up any doubt | Paid book |
| BBC Learning English — [The English We Speak](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/the-english-we-speak) | Short everyday-idiom episodes | Free |

> These are for everyday English, not ITP-specific prep. For official ITP practice, use the
> [reference Resources list](<../reference/Resources.md>) instead.
