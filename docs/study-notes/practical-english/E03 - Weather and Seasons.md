# E03 — Weather and Seasons

**Where it pays off:** Listening Part A small talk ("Looks like it's going to pour…"),
Reading passages on climate/geography, and general vocabulary breadth.
**Time to learn:** 15 min · **Target on the drill:** ≥ 10 / 12.

> Low-yield for Structure, but weather is the single most common topic of English small talk,
> so it shows up in listening conversations. Learn the collocations as whole phrases.

---

## 1. Core weather vocabulary

| Word | Part of speech | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sunny / cloudy / rainy / windy / snowy / foggy | adjectives | *-y* turns the noun into "full of" |
| rain, snow, wind, fog, hail, drizzle | noun **and** verb | *It's raining.* / *The rain stopped.* |
| storm, thunderstorm, shower, breeze | nouns | *a light breeze*, *a heavy shower* |
| forecast | noun/verb | *the weather forecast says…* |
| temperature | noun | *the temperature dropped* |
| humid / dry | adjectives | tropical climates are *humid* |
| freezing / chilly / cool / mild / warm / hot / boiling | adjectives (cold → hot) | intensity scale |

## 2. Natural collocations — say them this way

| Say | Not |
|---|---|
| **It's raining.** / It's pouring. (raining hard) | ~~There is rain happening~~ |
| **It's going to rain.** | ~~It will rain soon maybe~~ |
| **heavy rain / light rain** | ~~big rain / small rain~~ |
| **strong wind / a gentle breeze** | ~~big wind~~ |
| **It's freezing / boiling (out there).** | — (emphatic cold/hot) |
| **What's the weather like?** | ~~How is the weather condition?~~ |
| **a nice / lovely / miserable day** | — |
| **The sun is out.** / **It cleared up.** | — (stopped raining) |

## 3. Temperature and intensity scale

`freezing → cold → chilly → cool → mild → warm → hot → boiling`

- *below freezing* = under 0°C · *mild* = pleasant, not extreme · *humid* = hot + wet air.
- Remember Celsius pronunciation from [E01](<E01 - Numbers, Decimals and Quantities.md>): *25°C = "twenty-five degrees."*

## 4. The four seasons

| Season | Months (N. hemisphere) | Adjective |
|---|---|---|
| spring | Mar–May | *spring weather* |
| summer | Jun–Aug | *a summer holiday* |
| autumn (UK) / fall (US) | Sep–Nov | *autumn leaves* |
| winter | Dec–Feb | *a winter coat* |

**Note:** seasons take **in** — *in (the) summer, in winter*. Many equatorial countries
(like Indonesia) have only *the dry season* and *the rainy / wet season* — both are correct English.

## Exercise — 12 items, 6 minutes

**Choose the natural word/phrase.**

1. It's really (pouring / big rain) out there — take an umbrella.
2. There's a (strong / big) wind today; hold your hat.
3. "What's the weather (like / condition)?"
4. The temperature (dropped / fell down) to below freezing last night.
5. It's (boiling / very much hot) today — about 38 degrees.
6. We get a lot of rain here in the (rainy season / rain time).
7. The forecast (says / tells) it'll clear up this afternoon.
8. A light (breeze / wind) is pleasant on a warm day.
9. In (the autumn / autumn time), the leaves turn brown.
10. It was only a light (shower / small rain), so we kept walking.
11. Finally the sun is (out / open) after three cloudy days.
12. Seasons: we say "in ____ summer" or "in summer"? → in ______ summer / in summer (both fine); which preposition? ______
