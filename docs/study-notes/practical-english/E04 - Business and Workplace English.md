# E04 — Business and Workplace English

**Where it pays off:** Reading passages (economics/management are common ITP topics),
the Structure/vocabulary overlap, and real-world use. Pairs with the
[Academic Word List](<../vocabulary/Academic Word List.md>) (many AWL words — *finance,
contract, sector, revenue* — are business words).
**Time to learn:** 20 min · **Target on the drill:** ≥ 12 / 15.

> The ITP tests **formal, standard** business English, not startup jargon. Learn the word
> *and* its common collocation — these words are almost never tested alone.

---

## 1. Core business nouns

| Word | Meaning | Common collocation |
|---|---|---|
| revenue | money coming in | *generate / increase revenue* |
| profit ↔ loss | money gained ↔ lost | *make a profit*, *run at a loss* |
| budget | planned spending | *stay within budget*, *cut the budget* |
| invoice | a bill for goods/services | *send / pay an invoice* |
| deadline | the final date | *meet / miss a deadline* |
| contract | a binding agreement | *sign / renew a contract* |
| supplier ↔ client/customer | who sells to you ↔ who buys | *a reliable supplier* |
| stakeholder | anyone affected by the business | *key stakeholders* |
| merger ↔ acquisition | combining ↔ buying a company | *a merger between X and Y* |
| shares / stock | ownership units of a company | *the share price rose* |

## 2. Core business verbs & phrasal verbs

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| launch | release to market | *launch a new product* |
| negotiate | discuss to reach a deal | *negotiate the terms* |
| outsource | hire an outside firm | *outsource IT support* |
| expand ↔ downsize | grow ↔ shrink | *expand into Asia*, *downsize the team* |
| **set up** | establish | *set up a meeting / a company* |
| **take over** | gain control of | *take over a rival* |
| **lay off** | dismiss for business reasons | *lay off 200 workers* |
| **carry out** | perform / conduct | *carry out market research* |
| **follow up** | check back on something | *follow up on the email* |

## 3. Meeting & email phrases (formal register)

| Function | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Arrange | *Could we schedule a meeting for Thursday?* |
| Propose | *I'd like to suggest / propose that we…* |
| Agree | *That works for me.* / *I'm in favour of that.* |
| Disagree politely | *I see your point, but…* / *I'm not sure that's feasible.* |
| Email opening | *I am writing to enquire about…* / *Further to our conversation…* |
| Email closing | *Please let me know if you have any questions.* / *Kind regards,* |
| Deadline chase | *Just following up on my previous email.* |

## 4. Business idioms (common, still formal enough for Reading)

| Idiom | Meaning |
|---|---|
| the bottom line | the final total / the key point |
| ballpark figure | a rough estimate |
| in the black ↔ in the red | profitable ↔ losing money |
| cut corners | do cheaply/carelessly |
| touch base | make brief contact |
| get the ball rolling | start something |

## Exercise — 15 items, 8 minutes

**Choose the correct word (1–10).**

1. We need to (meet / miss) the deadline by Friday — don't be late.
2. The company made a (profit / loss) of $2 million last quarter — a great year.
3. Please (sign / write) the contract and return one copy.
4. They decided to (lay off / set off) 50 employees after revenue fell.
5. Let's (set up / set in) a meeting for Monday.
6. A reliable (supplier / client) delivers goods on time.
7. The two firms announced a (merger / budget) to form one company.
8. Can you give me a (ballpark / football) figure for the cost?
9. After three losing years, the firm is finally in the (black / white).
10. We'll (carry out / carry on) market research before launching.

**Fix the register — rewrite more formally (11–15).**

11. "Gimme the numbers by tmrw." →
12. "Hey, wanna meet up Thursday?" →
13. "That's a no from me." →
14. "I'll check back later." →
15. "We're broke this quarter." →

## Answer key

| # | Answer | # | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | meet | 9 | black |
| 2 | profit | 10 | carry out |
| 3 | sign | 11 | *Could you send me the figures by tomorrow?* |
| 4 | lay off | 12 | *Could we schedule a meeting for Thursday?* |
| 5 | set up | 13 | *I'm not in favour of that.* / *I'm afraid I can't agree.* |
| 6 | supplier | 14 | *I'll follow up later.* |
| 7 | merger | 15 | *We're running at a loss this quarter.* |
| 8 | ballpark | | |
