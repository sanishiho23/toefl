# E08 — Everyday Collocations: *do · make · have · take* ⭐

**Where it pays off:** Listening Part A, Structure (wrong-verb errors), Reading, and all
real-world English. Collocation errors (*~~make the dishes~~*, *~~do a mistake~~*) are extremely
common for Indonesian speakers because one Indonesian verb (*melakukan, membuat*) maps to
several English ones.
**Time to learn:** 20 min · **Target on the drill:** ≥ 16 / 20.

> There is **no rule** — these are fixed partnerships you memorise. Learn the verb *with* its
> noun, as one unit, exactly like the word families in [S08](<../structure/S08 - Word Forms, Articles and Prepositions.md>).

---

## 1. DO — tasks, work, and vague/general activities

| do the... | do (a/an/some)... |
|---|---|
| do the laundry · do the dishes · do the shopping | do work · do business · do a job |
| do the housework · do the cleaning · do the cooking | do homework · do an assignment · do research |
| do the washing-up · do the ironing | do exercise · do a favour · do your best |
| do your hair · do the gardening | do damage · do harm · do nothing |

*Rough sense: DO = perform a task or activity, often routine or unspecified.*

## 2. MAKE — creating, producing, a result

| make a... | make (the/an)... |
|---|---|
| make a decision · make a choice · make a plan | make the bed · make an effort |
| make a mistake · make a mess · make a noise | make an appointment · make an excuse |
| make a phone call · make a reservation · make a booking | make an offer · make a profit |
| make a suggestion · make a promise · make a point | make money · make friends · make progress |

*Rough sense: MAKE = create or produce something — a result comes out of it.*

**The classic confusions:**
- **do** the dishes (wash them) ≠ **make** the dinner (create the meal)
- **do** homework ≠ **make** a mistake
- **make** a decision, never ~~do a decision~~ · **make** progress, never ~~do progress~~

## 3. HAVE — experiences, meals, relationships

| have a... | have (an)... |
|---|---|
| have a shower/bath · have a rest · have a break | have breakfast/lunch/dinner |
| have a party · have a meeting · have a chat | have a problem · have an idea |
| have a look · have a go/try · have a good time | have an argument · have an accident |
| have a cold · have a headache · have a baby | have a conversation · have fun |

*Rough sense: HAVE = experience something or consume a meal.*

## 4. TAKE — time, transport, actions you "pick up"

| take a... | take (an/the)... |
|---|---|
| take a break · take a shower · take a nap | take an exam/test · take a course |
| take a photo · take a seat · take a look | take the bus/train/a taxi |
| take a chance/risk · take a walk · take a trip | take medicine · take notes |
| take a message · take time · take care | take responsibility · take part (in) |

*Rough sense: TAKE = time/transport, or an action you "pick up and perform."*

**Overlap traps:**
- **have** a shower (UK) ≈ **take** a shower (US) — both correct.
- **take** an exam = sit it · **do** well on an exam = your result · **make** the grade = succeed.

## Exercise — 20 items, 10 minutes

**Choose do / make / have / take (1–14).**

1. I need to ______ the laundry before the weekend.
2. She has to ______ an important decision today.
3. Let's ______ a break for ten minutes.
4. Did you ______ breakfast this morning?
5. He ______ a mistake on the form.
6. Could you ______ me a favour?
7. They're going to ______ a reservation for Friday.
8. I always ______ notes during lectures.
9. We ______ a lot of fun at the party.
10. You should ______ more exercise.
11. Can I ______ a look at your notes?
12. The students will ______ the exam next week.
13. Please ______ an appointment with the doctor.
14. I have to ______ my homework tonight.

**Fix the collocation error (15–20).**

15. ❌ *She made the dishes after dinner.* →
16. ❌ *He did a big mistake.* →
17. ❌ *We did a party last night.* →
18. ❌ *I must do a decision soon.* →
19. ❌ *Let's make a photo.* →
20. ❌ *They are doing progress in English.* →
