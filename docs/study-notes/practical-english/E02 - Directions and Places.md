# E02 — Directions and Places ⭐

**Where it pays off:** Listening Part A (campus conversations: "Where's the library?",
"How do I get to…?") **and** Structure S08 prepositions. Prepositions of place are also
the second-most-missed error type in Written Expression after word forms.
**Time to learn:** 20 min · **Target on the drill:** ≥ 12 / 15.

> Indonesian collapses many of these into *di* / *ke*. English forces a choice —
> *in / on / at / by / next to* — and the test lives in that choice.

---

## 1. Prepositions of place — the core set

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **in** | inside a space/area | *in the classroom, in Jakarta, in the box* |
| **on** | touching a surface / a line | *on the table, on the wall, on the second floor* |
| **at** | a specific point / event | *at the door, at the bus stop, at the meeting* |
| **next to / beside** | directly at the side of | *The café is **next to** the bank.* |
| **between** | in the middle of two things | *between the library and the gym* |
| **in front of** | facing the front of | *Park **in front of** the building.* |
| **behind** | at the back of | *The parking lot is **behind** the hall.* |
| **across from / opposite** | on the other side, facing | *The post office is **across from** the station.* |
| **near / close to** | a short distance away | *a hotel **near** the airport* |
| **above / over** | higher than | *a lamp **above** the desk* |
| **below / under** | lower than | *a shop **below** the office* |
| **inside / outside** | within / beyond the walls | *wait **outside** the office* |

**Common fix — "beside" vs "besides":**
*beside* = next to (place). *besides* = in addition to. ❌ *beside of* — there is no *of*.

## 2. Floors and buildings — the ones learners get wrong

| Correct | Note |
|---|---|
| **upstairs / downstairs** | one word, **no** *to* or *the*: *go **upstairs*** (❌ *go to upstairs*) |
| **on the ground floor** (UK) / **on the first floor** (US) | UK ground = US first; watch the gap |
| **on the top floor / on the second floor** | floors take **on** |
| **up the stairs / down the stairs** | the physical staircase (with *the*) |
| **at the end of the hall / corridor** | *at the end of*, not ~~in the end of~~ |
| **around the corner** | just past the corner |

## 3. Giving and following directions

| Phrase | Means |
|---|---|
| **go straight (ahead) / go down this street** | continue forward |
| **turn left / turn right (at the corner)** | change direction |
| **take the first/second left** | the first/second turning on the left |
| **go past the bank** | continue beyond it |
| **it's on your left / right** | the side it appears on |
| **go two blocks and…** | pass two street sections |
| **it's just around the corner** | very close, past the corner |

## 4. Movement prepositions (to / into / onto / through)

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **to** | direction toward | *walk **to** the station* |
| **into** | entering an enclosed space | *go **into** the room* |
| **onto** | moving to a surface | *step **onto** the platform* |
| **through** | in one side and out the other | *go **through** the gate* |
| **along** | following a line | *walk **along** the river* |
| **toward(s)** | in the direction of (not necessarily reaching) | *head **toward** the tower* |

**Trap:** *arrive* takes **in** (city/country) or **at** (building/event), never ~~arrive to~~.
See the fixed-preposition table in [S08](<../structure/S08 - Word Forms, Articles and Prepositions.md>).

## Exercise — 15 items, 8 minutes

**Fill the gap with one preposition / direction word.**

1. The bookshop is right ______ to the pharmacy.
2. Don't wait here — the bathroom is ______ (one floor down).
3. Our office is ______ the third floor.
4. The bus stop is ______ from the hospital, on the other side of the road.
5. Park your car ______ the building, at the back.
6. She lives in the house ______ the bakery and the school.
7. Go ______ ahead and turn ______ at the second corner.
8. The elevator is ______ the end of the corridor.
9. He walked ______ the room and sat down. (entering)
10. The lecture hall is ______ of the library — you'll see it as you approach.
11. Take the ______ left and the station is on your ______.
12. ❌ *Please wait beside of the door.* → fix: ______
13. ❌ *I'll go to upstairs.* → fix: ______
14. We arrived ______ the airport two hours early.
15. The gym is just ______ the corner from here.
