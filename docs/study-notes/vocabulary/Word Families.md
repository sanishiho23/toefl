# Word Families — the S08 cheat code

**Why this file exists:** Written Expression (25 of 40 questions) asks you to spot a wrong word
form. If you know a family as a *block*, the answer is instant. If you know the words one at a
time, you'll guess.

**How to study:** cover the right three columns, say them out loud, check. 20 minutes.
