# S08 — Word Forms, Articles, Prepositions, Pronouns ⭐

**Section:** Written Expression (25 of the 40 questions) · **Est. yield:** 8–12 questions
**Time to learn:** 30 min · **This is the highest-value module in the course.**

> These questions have no rule you can reason your way to. They test whether you *know* that
> `decision` is a noun and `decisive` is an adjective. It looks like grammar; **it is vocabulary.**
> A wrong word form is the single most common error type in Section 2.

---

## 1. The suffix table — memorise it cold

| Part of speech | Common suffixes | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Noun** | -tion, -sion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -ance/-ence, -er/-or, -ist, -dom, -ship, -th, -al | decision, government, happiness, ability, importance, teacher, scientist, freedom, leadership, growth, approval |
| **Verb** | -ate, -ify, -ize/-ise, -en, en- | create, simplify, organize, widen, enable |
| **Adjective** | -ive, -able/-ible, -ful, -less, -ous, -al/-ical, -ic, -ary, -ent/-ant, -ed, -ing | decisive, reliable, careful, useless, famous, historical, economic, primary, different |
| **Adverb** | -ly (adjective + ly) | decisively, carefully, historically |

### Word families — learn them as blocks, never as isolated words
```
decide (v) → decision (n) → decisive (adj) → decisively (adv) → indecisive (adj)
analyse (v) → analysis (n) → analytical (adj) → analytically (adv)
succeed (v) → success (n) → successful (adj) → successfully (adv) → unsuccessful (adj)
vary (v) → variety (n) → various (adj) → variation (n) → invariably (adv)
compete (v) → competition (n) → competitive (adj) → competitor (n) → competitively (adv)
```
Full list: [Word Families](<vocabulary/[16-Oct] Word Families.md>)

### How to decide the answer — the slot test
1. Look at the **slot**, not the word.
2. Article (*a/an/the*) or possessive → **noun**.
3. After *very / more / most / extremely* → **adjective**.
4. Modifying a verb / adjective / whole sentence → **adverb**.
5. Slot needs an action → **verb**, and check its tense/agreement.

```
Her ___ surprised everyone.        → decision (noun)
The ___ of the court was final.    → decision (noun)
She is very ___ .                  → decisive (adjective after very)
She acted ___ .                    → decisively (adverb modifying a verb)
```

## 2. Articles & determiners

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| **a / an** = one of many, first mention | *a* university, *an* hour (sound, not letter) |
| **the** = both speaker and listener know which | *the* report we discussed |
| **no article** for general plurals & uncountables | *Information* is valuable. *Students* need sleep. |
| **no article** for most proper nouns | *Japan, Professor Smith, Mount Fuji* |
| **the** with unique things, superlatives, ordinals, decades | *the* sun, *the* best, *the* first, *the* 1990s |
| **the** with *of*-phrases | *the* structure *of* the atom |

**Count vs non-count — these are always non-count:**
`information, advice, research, equipment, furniture, luggage, news, homework, knowledge,
money, traffic, weather, water, evidence, progress, work, education, health, behaviour`
❌ *an information* · ❌ *informations* · ❌ *many research*

**Quantity words:**
- countable → *many, few, a few, number of, several*
- non-count → *much, little, a little, amount of, a great deal of*
- both → *some, a lot of, plenty of, most of, all of*

## 3. Prepositions — the fixed pairs that get tested

| Correct | Wrong |
|---|---|
| afraid **of**, capable **of**, aware **of**, consist **of**, accuse **of**, approve **of** | afraid **from** |
| interested **in**, successful **in**, result **in**, participate **in**, specialize **in** | interested **on** |
| depend **on**, rely **on**, based **on**, concentrate **on**, insist **on** | depend **of** |
| responsible **for**, famous **for**, known **for**, apply **for**, account **for** | responsible **to** |
| different **from**, protect **from**, prevent **from**, suffer **from**, result **from** | different **than** |
| similar **to**, according **to**, due **to**, superior **to**, refer **to**, response **to** | similar **with** |
| proud **of**, full **of**, regard **as**, regard **as**, consist **of** | proud **for** |
| arrive **in** (city/country) / arrive **at** (building/event) | arrive **to** |
| married **to**, compared **with/to**, consist **of**, composed **of** | married **with** |

**Time prepositions:** *on* Monday · *in* June / *in* 2020 · *at* 5 o'clock ·
*by* Friday (= not later than) · *during* the meeting · *for* two hours · *since* March

## 4. Pronouns

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| pronoun must agree with its antecedent | *Each student must bring **his or her** book.* |
| reflexive for emphasis or same-subject object | *She taught herself.* (not *She taught her.*) |
| use subject pronoun after *than/as* in formal writing | *He is taller than **I** (am).* |
| `who` = subject, `whom` = object | *the man **whom** we met* |
| don't repeat the subject with a relative pronoun | ❌ *the man who he called* |
| `one` / `that` / `those` for replacement | *the climate of Japan is milder than **that** of Canada* |
| indefinite pronouns are singular | *everyone **has**, somebody **is**, neither **was*** |

---

## 5. Redundancy & double negatives — free points

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| the reason is **because** | the reason is **that** |
| **return back**, **repeat again**, **cooperate together** | return, repeat, cooperate |
| **more** superior, **most** unique | superior, unique |
| **can't hardly**, **don't have no** | can hardly, don't have any |
| **may possibly**, **past history**, **final outcome** | may, history, outcome |
| **in my opinion I think** | In my opinion, … / I think … |

## Exercise — 15 items, 8 minutes

Each sentence has one underlined word that may be wrong. Choose the error, or "No error".

1. The committee reached a **decisive** after three hours of **discussion**.
   (A) decisive → should be **decision**
2. She analysed the data **extreme** carefully.  → **extremely** (adverb)
3. The **equipments** in the laboratory **were** expensive. → **equipment** (non-count)
4. His **success** in the field was due to **careful** planning. → No error
5. The professor is very **knowledge** about the subject. → **knowledgeable**
6. The results were **similar with** those of the previous study. → **similar to**
7. Each of the students must submit **their** assignment on time. → **his or her**
8. She is **responsible to** organizing the conference. → **responsible for**
9. The **informations** you requested **have** been sent. → **information … has**
10. The experiment was conducted **very careful**. → **very carefully**
11. It is **importance** to record every observation. → **important**
12. He arrived **to** the seminar **at** nine o'clock. → **arrived at**
13. The reason for the delay was **because** the samples were contaminated. → **that**
14. The new policy will **effect** all employees. → **affect** (verb) — *effect* is the noun
15. She has been working here **since** three years. → **for** three years
