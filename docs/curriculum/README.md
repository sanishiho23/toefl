---

## Why the curriculum is shaped like this

You said your weak section is **Structure & Written Expression**. Two consequences:

1. **Weeks 0–1 (14 days) go almost entirely to Structure.** It's only ~15 recurring grammar
   rules, it's the shortest section (25 min), and it's the single fastest place to buy points.
   Going from 20/40 to 32/40 is worth roughly **+40 total points**.
2. **Structure never disappears.** Weeks 2–5 keep a weekly maintenance drill, because grammar
   decay is real and this section punishes rust more than the others.

Reading and Listening get one full week each, then Week 4 welds them together under exam timing.

## Session anatomy — every day follows this shape

| Block | Minutes | What |
|---|---:|---|
| Warm-up | 5 | 10 flashcards from yesterday's misses |
| **Instruction** | 25 | Read the linked module. Write the rule in your own words. |
| **Guided practice** | 20 | Module exercises, untimed, check as you go |
| **Timed drill** | 25 | Section-timing. No pausing. |
| **Error review** | 20 | [error log](<../../progress/error-log.md>): rule → why → fix |
| **Log & commit** | 5 | Fill `docs/logs/daily/week-NN/DD-MM-YYYY.md`, `git commit` |

≈ **100 min.** On 90-min days, cut the guided-practice block, never the error review.

## If you fall behind

Do **not** compress by skipping days. Cut in this order:

1. Drop the "guided practice" block (keep instruction + timed drill + error review).
2. Skip the Sunday review day.
3. Merge Reading and Listening weeks into 4 shared days.
4. Never cut the error review. Never cut a mock exam.
