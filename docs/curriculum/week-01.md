## D8
**Mon 12 Oct · S04 — Noun clauses & reported speech** · 100 min

- **Material:** [S04   Noun Clauses and Reported Speech](<../study-notes/structure/[12-Oct] S04 - Noun Clauses and Reported Speech.md>)
- **Log:** `docs/logs/daily/week-01/12-10-2026.md`

| Block | Min | Task |
|---|---:|---|
| Warm-up | 5 | 10 flashcards from Week 00. |
| Instruction | 25 | Read S04. Core: a noun clause is **that/what/whether + SUBJECT + VERB** — never a question order. |
| Guided practice | 20 | S04 exercises (20 items). |
| Timed drill | 25 | Mixed S01–S04, 25 items, **15 min**. |
| Error review | 20 | Error log. |
| Commit | 5 | |

**Non-negotiables:** `I don't know where **he is**` (not *is he*) · `that` can be dropped after
*say/think/know* but **not** as a subject · `whether` ≈ `if` but never after a preposition.

**Success criteria:** ≥ 16/20.
