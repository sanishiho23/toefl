# Mock Exam Protocol

Run this on **Day 27 (Sat 31 Oct)** and **Day 32 (Thu 5 Nov)**. Do not improvise it.

---

## Before — 15 minutes

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Pick the time | Same time of day as your real exam if you know it. Your circadian rhythm affects listening scores. |
| Paper or screen | Match the format your institution uses. If paper, print the answer grids. |
| Remove all distractions | Phone **off**, in another room. No tabs. No music. |
| Water + light snack on the desk | You are allowed these in most sittings; practise with them. |
| Analogue watch | Not a phone. Not a smartwatch. |
| One bathroom break | Before you start, not during. |
| Write the start time on the grid | You need to know your true finish time. |

---

## The sitting — 115 minutes, straight through

| Order | Section | Questions | Time | Rules |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | Listening | 50 | **35 min** | Audio plays once. No pauses. Transfer answers in the stated interval. |
| 2 | Structure | 40 | **25 min** | Stop at 25:00 exactly. Record how far you got. |
| 3 | Reading | 50 | **55 min** | Stop at 55:00 exactly. Record how far you got. |

**Do not** take a break between sections beyond what a real sitting allows (~5 min admin).
**Do not** look at your phone between sections — that alone invalidates the listening result.

---

## Immediately after — 20 minutes

1. **Mark it.** Use the answer keys.
2. **Convert.** Count raw per section → [score table](<../study-notes/reference/Score Conversion.md>)
   → three section scores → `round((L+S+R) × 10/3)`.
3. **Write it down** in [score tracker](<../../progress/score-tracker.md>) before you
   analyse anything. Numbers first, opinions second.

### D. The three questions that matter

Answer these in writing, in your daily log:

1. **What was my single most expensive mistake pattern?** (Be specific: not "grammar" but
   "word-form errors after *very*".)
2. **Did I run out of time anywhere? Where exactly did the clock hurt me?**
3. **Which 10 questions would I get right next time with no extra study?** (These are careless
   errors — they are the cheapest points on the test.)

## Rules

- **Never retake the same mock within a week.** You'd be measuring memory, not ability.
- **Never stop a mock early.** Finishing badly teaches you more than quitting comfortably.
- **Never skip the analysis.** Sitting a mock without analysis is worth roughly zero.
