# Part A — Sentence completion (1–15)

1. The committee reached a ___ after three hours of debate.
   (A) decisive  (B) decision  (C) decisively  (D) decide
2. The effects of the policy on small businesses ___ still unknown.
   (A) is  (B) are  (C) has been  (D) was
3. She avoided ___ the question directly.
   (A) answer  (B) to answer  (C) answering  (D) answered
4. By the time the delegates arrived, the meeting ___ already begun.
   (A) has  (B) had  (C) was  (D) will have
5. I am not sure ___ the results can be reproduced.
   (A) that  (B) what  (C) whether  (D) which
6. The researcher ___ discovered the effect retired last year.
   (A) which  (B) who  (C) whose  (D) whom
7. If the samples ___ properly, the results would be reliable.
   (A) are stored  (B) were stored  (C) will be stored  (D) stored
8. Rarely ___ such a carefully designed experiment.
   (A) we have seen  (B) have we seen  (C) we saw  (D) did we saw
9. She is interested ___ marine biology.
   (A) on  (B) in  (C) at  (D) of
10. A number of proposals ___ submitted before the deadline.
    (A) was  (B) has been  (C) were  (D) has
11. ___ carefully, the experiment yields reliable results.
    (A) Doing  (B) To do  (C) Done  (D) Having done
12. All applications must ___ before the end of the month.
    (A) submit  (B) be submitted  (C) have submitted  (D) submitting
13. It is essential that every applicant ___ on time.
    (A) is  (B) be  (C) was  (D) will be
14. The population of the city is larger than ___ of the surrounding towns.
    (A) those  (B) that  (C) these  (D) it
15. The lecture was canceled ___ the professor's illness.
    (A) because  (B) because of  (C) although  (D) despite
