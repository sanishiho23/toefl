# S06 — Adverb Clauses, Conjunctions & Conditionals

**Section:** Structure & Written Expression · **Est. yield:** 3–5 questions
**Time to learn:** 25 min

---

## 1. The connector table — memorise by meaning

| Meaning | Conjunctions (+ clause) | Prepositions (+ noun phrase) |
|---|---|---|
| **Cause** | because, since, as | because of, due to, owing to |
| **Effect** | so … that, such … that | — |
| **Contrast** | although, though, even though, whereas, while | despite, in spite of |
| **Condition** | if, unless, provided that, as long as, in case, even if | — |
| **Time** | when, while, after, before, until, as soon as, once, by the time | after, before, during, until, since |
| **Purpose** | so that, in order that | in order to, for |
| **Manner** | as, as if, as though | like |

> **The single most common error type in this module: conjunction used where a preposition is needed.**
> `because` + a clause with a verb · `because of` + a noun phrase.
```
❌ The flight was canceled because the fog.     ✔ … because of the fog.
❌ Despite of the rain, we continued.           ✔ Despite the rain, …
```

---

## 2. Tense in time clauses — never future in the *when* clause
```
❌ I will call you when I will arrive.
✔  I will call you when I arrive.
```
Same for: *after, before, as soon as, until, by the time, if, unless, in case.*

---

## 3. Conditionals

| Type | If-clause | Main clause | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero | If + present | present | general truth: *If you heat water, it boils.* |
| First | If + present | will / can / may + base | real future: *If it rains, we will cancel.* |
| Second | If + **past** | would / could / might + base | unreal present: *If I knew, I would tell you.* |
| Third | If + **past perfect** | would have + p.p. | unreal past: *If I had known, I would have called.* |

⚠️ **Never `would` in the if-clause.** ❌ *If I would have known.*

**Mixed conditional:** *If I had studied* (past) , *I would be* ready now (present).

**Subjunctive `were`:** in second conditionals, use **were** for all persons.
```
If she ___ here, she would agree.   → were   (not "was")
```

**Inversion without `if`:**
```
___ I known about the delay, I would have come earlier.  → Had
Were she present, she would object. · Should you need help, call me.
```

---

## 4. `unless` = if … not
```
Unless you submit the form, you will not be considered.
= If you do not submit the form, you will not be considered.
```
⚠️ Don't add another negative: ❌ *Unless you don't submit…*

---

## 5. Contrast pairs worth drilling
- **although / though** + clause · **despite / in spite of** + noun or gerund
- **while / whereas** = direct contrast between two facts
- **however / nevertheless** are **adverbs**, not conjunctions — they need a semicolon or period:
  ✔ *The data was clear; however, the conclusion was disputed.*
  ❌ *The data was clear, however the conclusion…* (comma splice)

---

## 6. `so` vs `such`
```
so + adjective/adverb          → so difficult that …
such + (a/an) + adjective + noun → such a difficult exam that …
so + many/much/few/little + noun → so many problems that …
```

## Exercise — 12 items, 7 minutes

1. The lecture was canceled ___ the professor's illness.
   (A) because  (B) because of  (C) although  (D) despite
2. ___ she had little experience, she was hired immediately.
   (A) Despite  (B) Although  (C) In spite of  (D) Because of
3. If the samples ___ properly, the results would be reliable.
   (A) are stored  (B) were stored  (C) will be stored  (D) stored
4. I will send you the file as soon as I ___ it.
   (A) will finish  (B) finished  (C) finish  (D) am finishing
5. ___ I known about the deadline, I would have applied.
   (A) If  (B) Had  (C) Did  (D) Would
6. The experiment failed ___ the equipment was defective.
   (A) because  (B) because of  (C) due to  (D) despite
7. Unless the funding ___ , the project will end next month.
   (A) is renewed  (B) will be renewed  (C) renews  (D) renewed
8. If he ___ here now, he would know what to do.
   (A) is  (B) was  (C) were  (D) has been
9. The results were published; ___ , few researchers noticed them.
   (A) however  (B) although  (C) despite  (D) even though
10. It was ___ a complex problem that nobody solved it.
    (A) so  (B) such  (C) too  (D) very
11. ___ the heavy rain, the field trip was not canceled.
    (A) Although  (B) Because  (C) Despite  (D) Even though
12. If the researchers ___ the anomaly earlier, they would have changed the protocol.
    (A) notice  (B) noticed  (C) had noticed  (D) would notice
