# Score Conversion — TOEFL ITP Level 1

---

## 1. What is exact

Three facts from ETS that are not estimates:

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Total score range | **310 – 677** |
| Section score ranges | Listening 31–68 · Structure 31–68 · Reading 31–67 |
| Total from sections | **`Total = round((L + S + R) × 10 / 3)`** |

Check: minimum 31+31+31 = 93 → 93 × 10/3 = **310** ✔
Maximum 68+68+67 = 203 → 203 × 10/3 = 676.7 → **677** ✔

> **So: your three section scores must add to 165 for a total of 550.**
> That is the number to memorise. It is exact.

Example splits that give 550:

| Listening | Structure | Reading | Sum | Total |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 54 | 57 | 54 | 165 | 550 |
| 52 | 60 | 53 | 165 | 550 |
| 56 | 58 | 51 | 165 | 550 |
| 50 | 62 | 53 | 165 | 550 |

Note the second row: **a strong Structure section buys you slack everywhere else.**
That is why Weeks 0–1 exist.

## 3. What 550 actually requires

Using the estimate tables:

| Section | Raw needed | % correct |
|---|---:|---:|
| Listening | 38 / 50 | 76% |
| Structure | 32 / 40 | 80% |
| Reading | 35 / 50 | 70% |
| **Total** | **≈ 105 / 140** | **75%** |

**Cross-check:** L 38 → 56 · S 32 → 57 · R 35 → 54 · sum 167 → **557**. ✔

> You do **not** need to be brilliant. You need to be 75% accurate for two hours.
> That is a very reachable target, and it is why the plan is built on eliminating
> *careless* errors rather than learning exotic grammar.

## 4. Score → CEFR (as published by ETS)

| TOEFL ITP Level 1 | CEFR |
|---|---|
| 627–677 | C1 |
| 543–626 | B2 |
| 460–542 | B1 |
| 337–459 | A2 |
| 310–336 | A1 or below |

*(Levels as published by ETS; confirm against the certificate you receive.)*

**550 sits just inside B2.** Worth knowing if your institution states a CEFR requirement.

## 5. How to log a mock score

1. Count raw correct per section.
2. Convert with the tables above → three section scores.
3. Apply the exact formula → estimated total.
4. Write it in [../../progress/score-tracker.md](../../progress/score-tracker.md).

Label every estimated total with "(est.)" so you never confuse it with an official result.
